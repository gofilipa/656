\subsection{more research on word vectors}
\label{sec:org6e160f3}
Man is to Computer Programmer as Woman is to Homemaker? Debiasing Word
Embeddings. Bolukbasi et al 2016
\begin{itemize}
\item word embeddings reflect gender bias
\item technique called "debias" which removes the bias from the embedding,
"neutralizes" them.
\end{itemize}

Lipstick on a Pig: Debiasing Methods Cover up Systematic Gender Biases
in Word Embeddings But do not Remove Them, Hila Gonen, Yoav
Goldberg, 2019. 
\begin{itemize}
\item debias is like a bandaid, there's still bias in the embeddings.
\end{itemize}
